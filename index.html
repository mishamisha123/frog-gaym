1. The first 5,000 Froggy is unsecured. Every Froggy above that amount must be covered by selected collateral at its full Bank Value.

2. Characters, lakes, permanent vehicle models, paid game licenses, and selected Piggy savings may be pledged. Flights are never collateral.

3. Flights are consumables. They are never pledged, reserved, or seized by the Bank.

4. Loan money enters the normal wallet. There is no separate leverage toggle, tagged balance, loss surcharge, or automatic profit sweep.

5. The next fixed payment may be paid early and resets the five-round countdown. Early payoff removes unearned interest.

6. If overdue, only the assets listed above may be seized before any level penalty.

7. Froggy is fictional game currency—not real credit or cash.
